Who We Are

SIVAShiksha (Sumangali Institute of Valuable Arts), established in 2014, as a registered trust based in Delhi. For over a decade, SIVA Shiksha has been dedicated to harnessing India’s rich cultural heritage as a foundation for education, skilling, and empowerment. Our philosophy is rooted in the belief that culture is not just a legacy—it is a living force for social transformation. With the motto “Innovate in India, for India,” we design impactful programs that blend traditional Indian arts, literature, and storytelling with modern training and education practices.In 2026 the was converted into a foundation and registered as a 80G Company.

What We Do

SIVAShiksha’s mission is to build a future-ready, inclusive, and empowered society through:

  • Cultural Wisdom in Education: Enriching learning through storytelling, literature, and applied natya therapy pedagogy. Instilling pride, values, and identity in young learners.
  • Future-Ready Workforce: Offering training in digital literacy, vocational skills, and leadership to bridge the gap between education and employability.
  • Women’s Empowerment & Entrepreneurship: Providing mentorship, financial literacy, and access to markets to build women as independent earners and community leaders.
  • Careers for the Specially-Abled: Designing tailored skilling programs, career guidance, and advocating for inclusive workplaces to enable dignified employment.

Our Team

At the heart of SIVAShiksha is a team of educators, trainers, artists, and social innovators who share a common purpose: to use India’s cultural strengths as tools of empowerment. This collective is led by Dr. Dimple Kaur, PhD, whose vision integrates academic depth with lived social impact. Her leadership brings together research, empathy, and action to create meaningful and scalable change. Over the years, our team has partnered with corporates, NGOs, and communities to multiply social value, while staying rooted in integrity, transparency, and impact.
