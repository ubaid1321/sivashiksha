SIVA believes that mental health is not a privilege but a necessity for every individual. As part of our mission to build healthier, more resilient communities, we provide a comprehensive range of mental health services designed to address diverse needs, from awareness to long-term care.

Awareness, Education & Advocacy

  • Community awareness campaigns in schools, colleges, and workplaces
  • Culturally sensitive workshops, art-based programs, and training sessions
  • Advocacy for inclusive policies and recognition of mental health in public health discourse

Counseling & Psychotherapy

  • One-on-one counseling for stress, anxiety, depression, and trauma
  • Group sessions and peer support networks
  • Focus on emotional regulation, behavior modification, and coping mechanisms
  • Child and adolescent counseling within schools and community setups

Crisis Intervention & Helplines

  • Helplines for suicide prevention and distress counseling
  • On-ground intervention for survivors of abuse, domestic violence, or disasters
  • Referral linkages to psychiatric and medical services when necessary

Rehabilitation & Long-Term Care

  • Psychosocial rehabilitation programs
  • Halfway homes and community-based support systems
  • Vocational training to restore dignity and independence
  • Shelters for homeless individuals with mental illness

Therapeutic Interventions & Follow-ups

  • Art-Based & Cultural Therapies
  • Individual & Group Counseling
  • Mindfulness & Self-Awareness Practices
  • Continuous Follow-up & Progress Support

Focused Programs for Vulnerable Groups

  • Women – survivors of violence, abuse, and trauma
  • Children & Adolescents – exam stress, learning difficulties, behavioral challenges
  • Elderly – dementia care and caregiver support
  • Substance Abuse Recovery – community and family-based de-addiction support

Training & Capacity Building

  • Teachers, volunteers, and caregivers in early detection of mental health issues
  • Local community leaders in mental health first aid
  • Peer counselors to sustain ongoing support networks

Relationships & Family Support

  • Helping families understand mental health conditions and provide empathetic support.
  • Workshops and sessions to improve interpersonal understanding and conflict resolution.
  • Providing tools, guidance, and peer networks for those supporting loved ones with mental health challenges.
  • Encouraging families to reduce stigma and foster inclusive, nurturing home environments.

Research & Knowledge Building

  • Conducting research on psychosocial challenges in rural and urban contexts
  • Documenting community models for scalable mental health solutions
  • Sharing insights with partners, policymakers, and the wider public

At SIVA, our work goes beyond treatment—we strive to create an ecosystem of awareness, acceptance, and action where every individual has the opportunity to live with dignity and mental well-being.
