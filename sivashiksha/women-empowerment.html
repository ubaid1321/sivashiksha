Women's Empowerment

Building Stronger Families, Healthier Communities, and a More Equitable Society

At SIVA, empowering women is the foundation for building stronger families, healthier communities, and a more equitable society. Our women empowerment programs are designed to enable women to discover their potential, claim their rights, and pursue economic and social independence with dignity.

Skill Development & Livelihood Training

  • Vocational training in arts, crafts, tailoring, and other income-generating activities
  • Digital literacy and financial literacy workshops to enhance employability
  • Entrepreneurial training and micro-enterprise development support

Education & Capacity Building

  • Functional literacy programs for women with little or no formal education
  • Leadership training to encourage women to take active roles in community decision-making
  • Awareness workshops on health, nutrition, and legal rights

Mental Health & Emotional Well-Being

  • Counseling for survivors of violence and abuse
  • Peer support groups for shared learning and resilience building
  • Confidence-building and self-expression through art, theatre, and creative mediums

Advocacy & Rights Awareness

  • Gender equality and women's rights at the community and policy levels
  • Awareness of laws related to domestic violence, workplace harassment, and gender justice
  • Community dialogues to challenge gender stereotypes and cultural barriers

Support for Vulnerable Women

  • Safe spaces and support networks for survivors of abuse and neglect
  • Rehabilitation and reintegration support for marginalized women
  • Guidance and referrals for legal, medical, and financial aid
  • Proactive & Reactive Support in Domestic Violence (DV)

Leadership & Entrepreneurial Empowerment

  • Hands-on workshops to develop business ideas, operational skills, and market strategies.
  • Guidance on budgeting, savings, and accessing funding or credit.
  • Connecting women with mentors, peer groups, and community support for sustained growth.
  • Encouraging women to take active roles in local development initiatives and decision-making.

At SIVA, women's empowerment is not a standalone program—it is woven into everything we do. From skill training to mental well-being, from advocacy to community mobilization, we strive to create an environment where every woman has the opportunity to lead a life of dignity, independence, and hope.
