SivaShiksha Privacy Policy

1. Introduction & Purpose

At SivaShiksha, your privacy and security are important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information. Our work covers education, mental health support, entrepreneurship, workforce readiness, future skills, women empowerment, and inclusion for people with disabilities, including blind users. We value transparency and want you to understand our data practices. By using our services, you agree to the practices described here. We follow ethical standards, legal requirements, and our CSR goals to create a safe, inclusive, and supportive learning environment for everyone. This policy covers all our digital platforms, mobile apps, in-person programs, and related services under the SivaShiksha brand.

2. Scope of Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to everyone who uses SivaShiksha services, including students, educators, program participants, partners, and visitors to our website or mobile apps. It covers how we collect, store, use, and share personal, non-personal, and sensitive information. This includes data from third-party tools, online surveys, offline workshops, and CSR activities. We follow Section 8 regulations and other laws for nonprofit educational organizations. We focus on making our services accessible, especially for people with disabilities and blind users. No matter which program you join, your information is protected by this policy. By explaining the scope, we aim to be transparent and build trust, showing our commitment to privacy, fairness, and ethical data use.

3. Information We Collect

SivaShiksha collects information to provide personalized educational services. This includes names, email addresses, phone numbers, demographic details, education, and work history. We collect sensitive information, like mental health needs or disability accommodations, only with your clear consent. We also gather non-personal data, such as IP addresses, browser types, and how you use our platform, to improve your experience. Program data may include assessments, progress, and participation records. We collect only what is needed, handle all data securely, and follow ethical standards. Our practices meet Section 8 rules, support our CSR goals, and help us offer inclusive programs for mental health, entrepreneurship, workforce development, women empowerment, and future skills.

4. Personal Identification Information

Personal Identification Information (PII) is any data that can identify you directly. At SivaShiksha, this includes your name, contact details, date of birth, demographic information, education and work history, and records of your participation in our programs. We only collect PII with your consent and use it to improve your learning experience, help you access programs, issue certificates, and keep you informed about our services and events. We take extra care with sensitive PII related to mental health, disabilities, and gender-focused programs. Our systems keep your data confidential and follow Section 8 rules and our CSR goals. You have the right to access, update, or delete your PII. We only share PII with third-party providers when necessary for our services and always under strict confidentiality.

5. Non-Personal Information

Non-personal information is data that does not identify you directly. This includes website analytics, IP addresses, device types, browser details, location trends, and how people use our platform. We use this data to improve our programs, support research, and enhance learning. It helps us develop our curriculum, offer more mental health support, and improve entrepreneurship and workforce programs. Non-personal data also helps us measure the impact of our CSR and inclusion efforts, especially for women and people with disabilities, including blind users. While this data is low-risk, we store it securely and use it responsibly to keep your trust.

6. Sensitive Data & Special Categories

SivaShiksha treats sensitive information with extra care and security. This includes mental health details, disability status, learning needs, special accommodations, and gender-focused support. We focus on making our services accessible for blind users. We collect this data only with your clear consent and always explain why we need it and how it will be used. We use sensitive information to personalize your learning, offer mental health support, ensure everyone can join our programs, and track progress in workforce and entrepreneurship initiatives. We never share sensitive data without your permission, unless required by law or needed for program delivery. All sensitive data is encrypted and stored securely, showing our commitment to ethics, Section 8 rules, and our CSR goals.

7. Information Collection Methods

We collect information through online registrations, course sign-ups, surveys, assessments, workshops, and our mobile apps. Most data comes directly from you through forms and questionnaires. We also gather some information automatically using analytics tools and third-party services to improve our offerings. For programs like mental health, disability support, entrepreneurship, and future skills, we may ask for extra details to give you the best support. We always explain why we collect information, how we store it, and how we use it. Our methods follow ethical standards, Section 8 rules, and our CSR values. We always ask for your clear consent when collecting sensitive or personal data, showing our commitment to privacy and inclusion.

8. Use of Collected Information

SivaShiksha uses the information we collect to improve your experience, enhance our programs, and help you reach your learning goals. We use data to personalize your learning, track your progress, support mental health and special needs, and create opportunities for workforce development and entrepreneurship. Non-personal data helps us design better courses, improve accessibility, and measure the impact of our CSR work. We also use your information to keep you updated about programs and offer new skill-building opportunities, always following Section 8 and other rules. Sensitive data, like disability or mental health details, is only used to support you and is never shared without your consent. By using data responsibly, we aim to create a welcoming and empowering environment for everyone, including women, blind users, and other marginalized groups.

9. User Consent & Rights

SivaShiksha asks for your clear consent before collecting any personal or sensitive information. We explain why we need your data, what we will do with it, and how it will be used. You can withdraw your consent at any time, and we make it easy for you to update or delete your information. You also have the right to access your data, fix any mistakes, and request a copy for your own use. We ensure our consent process is accessible for people with disabilities, blind users, and those in women empowerment programs. Our consent practices follow Section 8 rules and ethical standards for nonprofits. By being open about consent, we support your privacy, control, and trust in how we handle your data.

10. Data Sharing with Partners

SivaShiksha may share user information with trusted partners only when necessary for program delivery, research, or CSR initiatives. This includes educational institutions, training providers, workforce development organizations, mental health professionals, and technology service providers. Sensitive and personal information is shared only with explicit consent, ensuring confidentiality and compliance with Section 8 requirements. We collaborate with partners to enhance entrepreneurship programs, future skills training, and women empowerment initiatives. Data shared is strictly limited to purposes essential for delivering quality services and monitoring outcomes. Third-party agreements include stringent clauses regarding privacy, data protection, and ethical usage, reflecting our commitment to inclusive education, mental health support, and empowerment of individuals with disabilities, including blind users. All partnerships are periodically reviewed for compliance and accountability.

11. Third-Party Services & Analytics

SivaShiksha leverages third-party services and analytics tools to improve platform functionality, user engagement, and learning outcomes. Analytics providers help monitor program performance, user activity, and overall service effectiveness. Third-party integrations include tools for course management, communication, assessment, and accessibility enhancements, particularly for individuals who have disabilities. We ensure that these services adhere to strict data protection standards and do not access personal or sensitive data beyond what is necessary for their functions. Insights generated are often anonymized or aggregated to protect user identity. These analytics inform improvements in mental health support, entrepreneurship programs, workforce development, and future skills training. By responsibly utilizing third-party services, SivaSiksha enhances program quality while maintaining privacy, ethical compliance, and alignment with Section 8 regulations and CSR commitments.

12. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

SivaShiksha utilizes cookies and tracking technologies to enhance user experiences, streamline website navigation, and tailor learning journeys. Cookies help remember user preferences, login sessions, program progress, and accessibility settings, including screen reader configurations for users who are blind. Tracking tools allow us to analyze engagement trends, measure participation in workshops, and monitor the effectiveness of CSR initiatives. Users have the option to accept, decline, or customize cookie settings through their browser or platform interface. Data collected via cookies is never sold and is stored securely, with strict access controls. These practices comply with applicable privacy regulations and Section 8 standards. Transparent use of cookies ensures participants benefit from a seamless, inclusive, and secure digital environment while protecting personal and sensitive information.

13. Data Retention Policy

SivaShiksha retains user data only as long as necessary to fulfill program objectives, legal obligations, and organizational purposes. Personal and sensitive data, including mental health information, disability details, and participation in women's empowerment programs, is stored for the duration required to deliver services effectively. After this period, information is securely archived or deleted in compliance with regulatory standards and Section 8 requirements. Retention schedules are reviewed periodically to ensure relevance and necessity. Aggregated non-personal data may be retained longer for research, curriculum development, and impact analysis. Retaining data responsibly supports continuous improvement in workforce development, entrepreneurship programs, future skills training, and CSR initiatives, while protecting privacy, fostering trust, and promoting ethical and inclusive practices.

14. Data Security Measures

SivaShiksha employs robust technical and organizational measures to protect user data against unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. Security protocols include encryption, secure servers, multi-factor authentication, regular vulnerability assessments, and restricted access based on role and necessity. Special attention is given to sensitive information, such as mental health records, disability data, and personal identifiers. Accessibility and inclusion measures ensure blind users and participants with special needs can engage safely with our platforms. Our security practices align with Section 8 compliance, ethical standards, and CSR principles, reflecting our commitment to the safe, reliable, and responsible handling of all data. Continuous monitoring and staff training reinforce security awareness and risk mitigation across all SivaSiksha programs and services.

Users have the right to access their personal and sensitive information stored by SivaShiksha. Access requests can be submitted through our platform, via email, or through our support channels. Once verified, users may review, update, or correct inaccuracies in their data. Special provisions are available to accommodate blind users or individuals with disabilities who may require alternative formats or assistance. Access rights also cover program-specific records, assessments, and participation histories. Corrections are processed promptly, ensuring accurate and reliable information is maintained across mental health, entrepreneurship, workforce development, and future skills initiatives. This process reinforces transparency, user autonomy, and compliance with Section 8 regulations, ethical standards, and our broader CSR commitments.

SivaShiksha supports data portability and deletion requests in compliance with privacy regulations and ethical standards. Deletion requests allow users to remove personal and sensitive data, including records of participation in mental health programs, entrepreneurship initiatives, workforce development activities, or future skills training. Requests are processed promptly while ensuring minimal disruption to ongoing programs. Accessibility accommodations are provided for individuals who are blind and those with disabilities. Data portability and deletion rights reinforce user control, trust, and adherence to Section 8 regulations, demonstrating SivaSiksha’s commitment to privacy, inclusivity, and responsible data stewardship.
